Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

College Cards ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. What We Collect

When you use our app, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City
  • Login credentials (email + password)
  • Payment details (processed through Apple or Google)
  • App usage data (such as pages visited, features used, etc.)
  • Feedback or support messages

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide access to digital discount offers in your city
  • Process monthly subscriptions
  • Send important updates and notifications
  • Improve and personalize the app experience
  • Respond to user feedback and support requests
  • Monitor usage trends to improve performance

3. Payment Information

We do not collect or store your payment card information directly. All subscription payments are securely handled by Apple App Store or Google Play Store, depending on your device.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

We may share limited information with trusted partners who help us operate the app, such as:

  • App store payment processors
  • Cloud service providers (e.g., Firebase, if used)
  • Analytics tools (if implemented)

These partners are only allowed to use your data for the purpose of helping us deliver and improve our services.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@mycollegecards.com.
